Building My Website: A Conversation with AI

June 2025 Pessi Vilppolahti 5 min read

I've been working on my personal website for a while now, and today I had an interesting conversation with an AI assistant that helped me transform it from a simple page into something much more professional. I thought it would be worth documenting this journey, as it shows how AI can be a powerful collaborator in the creative process.

The Starting Point

My website started as a very basic HTML page. I had my name, a photo, some information about myself, and my contact details. It was functional, but not particularly impressive. The styling was minimal - just a simple container with some basic text formatting. It served its purpose, but I knew it could be better.

The First Request: Make It Fancy

I asked the AI to make my index.html look "really really fancy." What followed was quite spectacular. The AI transformed my simple page into something that looked like it belonged in a modern portfolio showcase. There were particle backgrounds, twinkling stars, glowing orbs, glassmorphism effects, and smooth animations everywhere.

Me: make the index.html look really really fancy
AI: *creates a spectacular cyber-futuristic design with particles, stars, and animations*

It was impressive, but perhaps a bit too much. The design was beautiful, but it didn't quite feel like "me." It was more of a tech demo than a professional portfolio.

The Iteration Process

What followed was a series of refinements. I asked for changes, the AI made them, and we went back and forth until we found something that worked. Here's how the conversation progressed:

Me: make it responsive too
AI: *adds comprehensive responsive design with mobile navigation*
Me: remove the top navigation entirely
AI: *removes all navigation elements*
Me: make it look professional without changing the actual content
AI: *creates a clean, professional design that maintains all content*

This back-and-forth was actually quite educational. Each iteration taught me more about what I actually wanted from my website. The AI was patient and accommodating, making changes quickly and accurately based on my feedback.

Adding the Blog

After we settled on a professional design for the main page, I decided I wanted to add a blog. Again, I turned to the AI for help. I asked for a simple HTML blog that would look unified with my homepage.

The AI created a complete blog system with:

  • A blog listing page that matches my homepage design
  • A template for creating new blog posts
  • Navigation between the homepage and blog
  • Consistent styling across all pages

And now, here we are - my first blog post, written with the assistance of the same AI that helped me build the website it's being published on.

Reflections on Working with AI

This experience has given me some interesting insights into working with AI:

  1. AI is a great collaborator. It doesn't replace your creativity; it enhances it. The AI can generate ideas and implementations quickly, but you're still the one making the decisions about what works and what doesn't.
  2. Iteration is key. Rarely will the first version be perfect. The ability to quickly iterate and refine is where AI really shines. It can make changes instantly based on your feedback.
  3. You learn by doing. Even though the AI is doing a lot of the technical work, I'm learning a tremendous amount by seeing how it solves problems and implements designs.
  4. It's okay to change your mind. The AI doesn't get frustrated if you change direction. It's happy to revert changes, try different approaches, or start over completely.

The Technical Details

For those who are interested in the technical side, here's what we ended up with:

  • Static HTML/CSS: No frameworks, no databases, just simple static files that load quickly and work everywhere.
  • Unified Design: The blog shares the same color scheme, typography, and styling as the main page, creating a cohesive experience.
  • Responsive Design: Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
  • Easy to Maintain: Adding new blog posts is as simple as copying a template and filling in the content.

The entire site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which means it's free, fast, and reliable. And since it's all static files, there's no server to maintain or database to manage.

I'm also planning to continue refining the website. There are always improvements to be made, and I'm sure the AI and I will have many more interesting conversations as we continue to develop it.

Conclusion

Building this website has been a rewarding experience. It's been a collaboration between me and an AI, and I think the result is something we can both be proud of. The website is professional, functional, and - most importantly - it feels like mine.

If you're reading this, I hope you'll check back often. And if you're a fellow developer thinking about using AI to help with your projects, I'd encourage you to give it a try. It might just surprise you with what it can do.
