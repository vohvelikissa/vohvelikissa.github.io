Reflections

This conversation demonstrates several interesting aspects of working with AI:

  1. The AI is eager to please. It often goes above and beyond what's requested, sometimes creating more elaborate solutions than necessary.
  2. Iteration is natural. The back-and-forth process of refinement is where AI really shines. It can make adjustments quickly and doesn't mind changing direction.
  3. Specificity matters. Vague requests like "make it fancy" lead to elaborate results, while specific requests like "center the image" lead to precise changes.
  4. The human is still in control. At every step, Pessi made the decisions about what to keep, what to change, and what direction to go.
  5. It's a conversation, not a command. The most productive interactions were dialogs, not one-way instructions.

The Final Result

After all these iterations, we ended up with:

  • A professional homepage with unified styling
  • A separate blog section with its own page
  • A separate projects section with its own page
  • Consistent navigation across all pages
  • This blog post documenting the entire journey

The website is clean, professional, and functional. It serves as a portfolio, a blog, and a demonstration of what can be accomplished through human-AI collaboration.
